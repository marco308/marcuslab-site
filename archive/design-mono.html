About

Engineer, maker, polo player — building the kind of small, sharp software I want to use myself.

I lead data-platform engineering in the payments world. Most of what's here is native Swift — I care a lot about apps that feel like they belong on the platform, respect your privacy, and don't nag you.

The rest is glue: Home Assistant integrations, little APIs, and whatever keeps my homelab humming. When I'm not at a keyboard I'm usually on a polo pony, which is how ChukkaTimer happened.

Native by default

iOS & macOS in Swift / SwiftUI — no cross-platform shortcuts.

Privacy-first

No analytics, no third-party SDKs. Self-hosting where it makes sense.

Open source

I ship in the open and contribute back where I can.

Homelab tinkerer

Everything here runs from a homelab on marcuslab.uk.
