I lead data-platform engineering in the payments world. Outside of that, I build the kind of small, sharp software I want to use myself.

Most of what's here is native Swift — I care a lot about apps that feel like they belong on the platform, respect your privacy, and don't nag you. The rest is glue: Home Assistant integrations, little APIs, and whatever keeps my homelab humming.

When I'm not at a keyboard I'm usually on a polo pony, which is how ChukkaTimer happened.
