zigbrew

a smart Sage Barista Express, in two build logs.

How my coffee machine joined the smart home. Part one: two Zigbee relays soldered across the power and grinder switches, so it warms up when I get in the shower and grinds when I walk into the kitchen. Part two: a load cell in a 3D-printed dosing ring, so it grinds by weight instead of time. Photos, wiring diagrams and every mistake along the way.

Printed

Pan lid generator

Seven glass lids lived in a heap in the pan cupboard and clattered every time the door shut. Now six hang on the inside of the door, each in its own printed hook and cradle on bars screwed across the frame. The generator does it for any door: enter your door, printer bed and lids, and it arranges them and renders every STL in the browser, plus a fitting sheet.
