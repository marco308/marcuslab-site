Builds03

zigbrew

a smart Sage Barista Express, in two build logs.

Part one: two Zigbee relays soldered across the power and grinder switches, so it warms up when I get in the shower and grinds when I walk into the kitchen. Part two: a load cell in a 3D-printed dosing ring, so it grinds by weight instead of time. Photos, wiring diagrams and every mistake along the way.

Builds04

Pan lid generator

Seven glass lids lived in a heap in the pan cupboard and clattered every time the door shut. Now six hang on the inside of the door. The generator does it for any door: enter your door, printer bed and lids, and it arranges them and renders every STL in the browser, plus a fitting sheet.

About

The person behind it.

I build small software I want to use myself, and lately some hardware too.

The apps are Swift, because I like software that feels at home on the iPhone and doesn't nag. The servers are Python, running on a homelab that's bigger than one house needs.

When I'm not at a keyboard I'm usually on a polo pony, which is where ChukkaTimer came from.
