Drawer lights carrier board

A bare circuit board for an ESP32-C6-DevKitC-1: 12 door-contact inputs and a 5 V data output for a 24 V addressable LED strip. The same board that runs my drawers.

  • You get1 bare board and 1 SN74AHCT125N level shifter chip, not soldered
  • Size90 × 66 mm, 2 layers, lead-free HASL, four M3 holes
  • Inputs12 door contacts on three 6-way terminals (GPIO0–3, 6, 7, 10, 11, 18–21)
  • OutputStrip data from GPIO22, shifted to 5 V, plus fused +24 V and 0 V
  • Power24 V in; a Traco TSR 1-2450 makes 5 V for the C6
